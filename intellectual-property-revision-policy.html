4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & REVISION POLICY.

SYNTH MEDIA AGENCY // EFFECTIVE DATE: 17 SEPTEMBER 2026

1. Terms 2. Payment & Refund 3. Privacy 4. IP & Revisions 5. Services

1. Purpose and Scope

This Intellectual Property & Revision Policy explains how ownership, usage rights, customer materials, Synth materials, third-party assets, AI-assisted content, project files, approvals and revisions are handled when customers purchase services from Synth Media Agency (“Synth”, “we”, “us” or “our”).

This Policy forms part of Synth’s Terms & Conditions and applies to creative, digital, technology, branding, web, video, graphic design, content and related services unless a written agreement for a particular project states otherwise.

2. Definitions

“Customer Materials” means materials supplied by the customer, including logos, photographs, videos, text, documents, brand assets, trademarks, designs, data and other content.

“Final Deliverable” means the completed work that Synth agrees to provide under the purchased package after the applicable production, review and approval process.

“Source Files” means editable working files, project files, raw footage, design files, layered files, source code, drafts, working documents or other underlying production materials, where applicable.

“Third-Party Materials” means fonts, stock assets, templates, music, footage, plugins, software, APIs, libraries, images or other materials owned or licensed by parties other than Synth or the customer.

3. Customer Ownership of Customer Materials

The customer retains ownership of Customer Materials that the customer lawfully provides to Synth.

By submitting Customer Materials, the customer grants Synth the permissions reasonably necessary to use, reproduce, edit, adapt, process, store and incorporate those materials solely for providing the requested services and related business administration.

The customer is responsible for ensuring that it has the necessary rights, permissions, licences and consents to provide the Customer Materials and authorise Synth to use them for the project.

4. Customer Responsibility for Third-Party Rights

A customer must not submit material that it does not have permission to use.

This includes copyrighted images, music, video, fonts, logos, photographs, text, software, trademarks and other protected materials.

Where a customer instructs Synth to use a particular third-party asset, the customer may be responsible for obtaining the required licence or permission unless Synth expressly agrees to obtain it as part of the purchased service.

5. Synth-Owned Materials

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, Synth retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property, proprietary processes, templates, systems, workflows, design methods, know-how, internal tools, reusable components, business materials, concepts and other materials that were not created exclusively as the customer’s Final Deliverable.

Payment for a service does not automatically transfer ownership of Synth’s pre-existing intellectual property or internal production systems to the customer.

6. Ownership and Licence to Final Deliverables

Subject to full payment and any applicable third-party restrictions, the customer will receive the rights to use the Final Deliverable for the purpose and manner for which the purchased service was provided.

Where the package or project agreement expressly provides for transfer of ownership or copyright in a particular Final Deliverable, that transfer will take effect only after full payment and subject to any third-party rights.

Where no express assignment is provided, Synth grants the customer a non-exclusive right to use the Final Deliverable for its intended business, personal, advertising or promotional purpose.

7. Source Files and Editable Materials

Source Files are not automatically included in every package.

Where a package specifically includes editable/source files, Synth will provide the applicable files as part of the agreed deliverables.

Where source files are not included, Synth retains the underlying working files and production materials unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Payment for a Final Deliverable does not automatically transfer ownership of internal project files, working files, raw footage, templates, source code or other production materials.

8. Third-Party Assets and Licences

Some Final Deliverables may include materials licensed from third parties.

Examples include stock images, music, fonts, templates, software, plugins, APIs, footage and other licensed assets.

Such materials remain subject to their applicable licence terms.

Synth cannot transfer rights that it does not own.

Where a third-party licence limits how a customer may use a Final Deliverable, the customer must comply with those limitations.

9. Fonts, Music, Stock, Templates and Similar Resources

Where Synth uses third-party fonts, music, stock assets, templates or similar resources, the applicable licence may determine how the customer can use the final work.

If a particular asset requires the customer to obtain a separate licence, Synth may inform the customer where reasonably practicable.

The customer should not assume that every third-party asset included in a Final Deliverable can be extracted, resold, sublicensed or reused independently.

10. Website, Software and Code

For web design, web development and technology services, ownership and licensing may apply differently to website designs, source code, frameworks, plugins, APIs, software, hosting configurations and third-party components.

Unless expressly agreed otherwise, payment for a website or technology service does not automatically transfer ownership of third-party software, frameworks, libraries, plugins, APIs, proprietary Synth tools or other materials that Synth does not own.

The customer receives the rights specifically agreed for the completed website or technology service, subject to applicable third-party licences.

11. AI-Assisted Work

Synth may use Artificial Intelligence tools and other automated technologies during the creative or technical production process.

AI may be used for concept development, image generation, video creation, copywriting, editing, research, automation, design assistance, software development or other purposes depending on the service.

AI-assisted content may be subject to limitations imposed by the relevant AI provider, including licensing terms, usage restrictions, output policies or other conditions.

Synth does not guarantee that every AI-assisted output is completely unique, exclusively generated for one customer, eligible for copyright registration, trademark registration or other intellectual property protection.

Where a customer supplies instructions or materials for AI-assisted production, the customer remains responsible for rights in those materials.

Where the project requires exclusive ownership, trademark registration, copyright registration or other legally protected exclusivity, the parties should agree on the requirements before production.

12. Originality and Intellectual Property Protection

Synth aims to create original work or appropriately licensed work for customers.

However, no creative agency can guarantee that a design, phrase, visual concept, name, logo, AI-assisted output or other creative element will never resemble material independently created by another person.

Unless expressly included as a separate service, Synth does not provide legal clearance, trademark searches, copyright registration, patent advice or formal intellectual property legal opinions.

13. Revisions Included in Packages

Each Synth package may include a specified number of revisions or a defined revision allowance.

Customers should review the package description before ordering to understand what is included.

A revision means a reasonable requested change to the agreed direction or delivered work within the scope of the purchased service.

A revision does not automatically include a complete change of concept, new project, new brief, additional deliverable or substantial redesign.

14. Revision Requests

Revision requests should be submitted through the customer’s registered communication channel or dashboard where available.

Customers should provide clear and consolidated instructions to reduce unnecessary delays.

Synth may ask the customer to clarify conflicting or incomplete revision instructions before making changes.

15. Changes Outside the Original Brief

If a customer materially changes the original brief after production has begun, requests a different concept, changes the target audience or purpose, adds new deliverables, or otherwise requests work outside the purchased scope, Synth may treat the request as additional work.

Additional work may require a revised quotation, additional payment or a new order before production continues.

16. Revisions After Approval

Once a customer has approved a Final Deliverable, further changes may be treated as additional work even if the package originally included revisions, particularly where the requested changes arise from a change of mind, a new direction, new information or a change to previously approved content.

This does not limit any remedy available where the delivered work materially fails to conform to the agreed service.

17. Customer Delays and Revision Windows

Where Synth requests information, approvals, materials or revision instructions from a customer, the project timeline may be paused or adjusted until the required response is received.

If a customer does not respond within a reasonable period, Synth may close, pause or reschedule the project in accordance with the applicable package and Terms & Conditions.

18. Approval of Work

Synth may require customer approval before a project is treated as completed and released.

Approval may be provided through the customer dashboard, registered email or another recognised communication channel.

After approval, the customer is responsible for checking that the approved work contains the information, spelling, names, contact details and other business information the customer intended to use, except where the issue is attributable to Synth’s failure to follow the approved brief.

19. Portfolio and Promotional Use

Unless the customer has expressly requested confidentiality or the parties have agreed otherwise in writing, Synth may display completed work as part of its portfolio, website, social media, case studies or promotional materials.

Where reasonably appropriate, Synth may identify the customer or business associated with the work.

Customers who require a project to remain confidential or not be used for promotional purposes should communicate this requirement before or during the order.

20. Confidential Projects

Where a customer informs Synth that a project is confidential, Synth will take reasonable steps to treat the relevant project information as confidential, subject to legal obligations, necessary service providers, security requirements and the customer’s own disclosure of the information.

Confidentiality does not automatically transfer ownership of intellectual property to the customer.

21. Unused Concepts and Drafts

Concepts, drafts, rejected alternatives, unused ideas, internal mock-ups and working materials that are not part of the Final Deliverable remain with Synth unless otherwise agreed.

A customer’s payment for a package does not automatically grant rights to every concept or unused draft produced during the creative process.

22. Customer’s Right to Use the Final Work

After full payment and subject to third-party restrictions, the customer may use the Final Deliverable for the ordinary business, personal, advertising or promotional purposes for which it was purchased.

The customer should not resell, sublicense, distribute as a template, claim ownership of Synth-owned underlying tools or remove required third-party attribution or licensing notices where applicable.

23. Synth’s Right to Protect Its Work

Synth may retain copies of project records and deliverables for legitimate business, legal, security, backup and portfolio purposes, subject to the Privacy Policy and any confidentiality obligations.

Synth may take reasonable steps to protect its intellectual property where it believes its proprietary materials, source files, templates or other protected materials are being used outside the rights granted under the applicable agreement.

24. Infringement Claims

If a customer believes that a Final Deliverable improperly uses material belonging to another person, the customer should notify Synth promptly with the relevant details.

Synth may investigate, replace, modify or remove the disputed element where reasonably appropriate.

A customer who receives a third-party intellectual property complaint relating to Customer Materials or customer-directed content should notify Synth promptly and cooperate with any reasonable investigation.

25. No Automatic Transfer of Third-Party Rights

Nothing in this Policy transfers ownership of third-party intellectual property to Synth or the customer where those rights belong to another person or organisation.

Third-party rights continue to be governed by the relevant licence, contract or applicable law.

26. Relationship With Other Synth Policies

This Policy should be read together with Synth Media Agency’s Terms & Conditions, Payment, Cancellation & Refund Policy, Privacy Policy and the applicable service package description.

Where a written project agreement specifically addresses intellectual property or revisions, that project-specific agreement will govern the relevant matter to the extent of any inconsistency.

27. Changes to This Policy

Synth may update this Policy when its services, production methods, technology or legal requirements change.

The version applicable to an order will generally be the version in effect when the order was placed, unless a later change is required by law or otherwise agreed with the customer.
