3. PRIVACY POLICY.

SYNTH MEDIA AGENCY // EFFECTIVE DATE: 17 SEPTEMBER 2026

1. Terms 2. Payment & Refund 3. Privacy 4. IP & Revisions 5. Services

1. Introduction

Synth Media Agency (“Synth”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects the privacy of customers, website visitors, users and other individuals whose personal information we process.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Synth may collect, how we collect it, why we use it, how we protect it, when we may share it, and the rights available to individuals under applicable data-protection law.

This Policy applies to personal information collected through the Synth website, customer account system, service-ordering system, customer dashboard, communications, project delivery processes and related digital services.

2. Personal Data We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with Synth, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account/login information
  • Business or organization information
  • Billing and transaction information
  • Order and service information
  • Project briefs and descriptions
  • Uploaded files and materials
  • Communication records
  • Feedback and reviews
  • Website and dashboard usage information
  • Device and technical information
  • IP address and similar technical identifiers
  • Information required to provide requested services
  • Other information you voluntarily provide to us

We do not intentionally collect personal information that is unnecessary for providing our services or operating our business.

3. How We Collect Personal Data

We may collect personal information when you:

  • Visit or use our website
  • Create a customer account
  • Place an order
  • Complete a service form
  • Submit a project brief
  • Upload files or materials
  • Make a payment
  • Contact us by email or another official communication channel
  • Use our customer dashboard
  • Provide feedback or a testimonial
  • Interact with our website, systems or digital communications

We may also receive certain information from third-party service providers where necessary to provide services or process transactions.

4. Why We Use Personal Data

Synth may use personal information for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing customer accounts
  • Processing service orders
  • Providing requested services
  • Communicating with customers
  • Processing and confirming payments
  • Delivering completed projects
  • Providing access to customer dashboards
  • Managing revisions and project requirements
  • Responding to customer questions and complaints
  • Maintaining records
  • Improving our services and systems
  • Preventing fraud, abuse and unauthorized activity
  • Maintaining website and platform security
  • Meeting legal and regulatory obligations
  • Protecting our rights and property
  • Sending service-related communications
  • Sending marketing communications where permitted and appropriate

5. Lawful Basis for Processing

Where applicable, Synth processes personal data on lawful grounds recognized under applicable data-protection law.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

  • Performance of a contract or steps taken at the customer’s request before entering into a contract
  • Compliance with legal or regulatory obligations
  • Consent
  • Legitimate interests, where permitted by applicable law
  • Protection of rights, security or other lawful interests

Where consent is required, Synth will seek consent in an appropriate manner.

6. Customer and Project Materials

Customers may provide Synth with information, documents, images, videos, logos, brand materials, text, business information and other project materials.

Synth may use these materials to provide the requested service.

Customers are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights, permissions and lawful authority to provide such materials to Synth and authorize their use for the project.

Customers should not provide unnecessary sensitive personal information when submitting project materials.

7. Payment Information

Payments made through the Synth website may be processed by third-party payment providers such as Paystack.

Where the payment provider handles card processing, Synth does not directly store customers’ full card numbers or complete card-security credentials.

Synth may receive information such as payment status, transaction reference, amount, date, payment method and other information necessary to confirm and administer the transaction.

Customers should never send their card PIN, OTP, CVV, password or other confidential payment credentials to Synth.

8. Third-Party Service Providers

Synth may use third-party service providers to operate and deliver its services.

These may include: Payment processors; Website hosting providers; Cloud storage providers; Email providers; Social media platforms; Analytics services; Software and technology providers; AI and creative technology providers; Web development and infrastructure providers; and Other service providers necessary for business operations.

These providers may process information on behalf of Synth or independently under their own terms and privacy policies, depending on the nature of the service.

Synth will take reasonable steps to use appropriate service providers and protect personal data in accordance with applicable obligations.

9. Artificial Intelligence and Creative Technology

Synth may use Artificial Intelligence and other technology tools to assist with certain creative, digital, marketing, technical and administrative processes.

Depending on the service, information supplied by a customer may be processed through an AI or technology provider where reasonably necessary to provide the requested service.

Synth will take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessarily submitting sensitive personal information to AI systems or other third-party technology providers.

Customers should avoid submitting sensitive personal information that is not necessary for their project.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Synth may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, security, analytics, preferences and other legitimate website operations.

Cookies may help us understand how visitors use our website and improve the user experience.

Where required, Synth may provide appropriate cookie notices, controls or consent mechanisms.

11. Marketing Communications

Synth may use customer contact information to send service-related communications such as order confirmations, project updates, delivery notices, account notices and important service information.

Where permitted by law, Synth may also send promotional or marketing communications.

Customers may opt out of marketing communications where an appropriate unsubscribe or opt-out mechanism is provided.

Opting out of marketing communications does not prevent Synth from sending necessary transactional, security or service-related messages.

12. Sharing and Disclosure of Personal Data

Synth may disclose personal information where reasonably necessary for legitimate business purposes or as required or permitted by applicable law.

This may include sharing information with: Payment providers; Hosting and cloud-storage providers; Technology and software providers; Email and communication providers; AI or creative technology providers; Professional advisers; Service providers working with Synth; Law enforcement or regulatory authorities where legally required; Other parties where the customer has authorized the disclosure or applicable law permits it.

Synth does not sell customer personal information merely for advertising purposes.

13. International Data Transfers

Some third-party providers used by Synth may process or store information outside Nigeria.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, Synth will take reasonable steps to ensure that the transfer and processing are handled in accordance with applicable data-protection requirements.

14. Data Security

Synth takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

Security measures may include account controls, access restrictions, authentication procedures, secure service providers and other appropriate safeguards.

However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Customers are responsible for keeping their account credentials confidential and should notify Synth if they suspect unauthorized access.

15. Data Retention

Synth may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to: Provide services; Maintain customer accounts; Complete transactions; Manage projects; Resolve complaints or disputes; Maintain business and financial records; Meet legal or regulatory obligations; Prevent fraud and abuse; Maintain backups and security; Protect legitimate business interests.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it is held.

When information is no longer reasonably required, Synth may delete, anonymize or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

16. Data Subject Rights

Subject to applicable law and lawful limitations, individuals may have rights relating to their personal data under the NDPA (Nigeria Data Protection Act):

  • Request access to personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion where legally applicable
  • Request restriction of processing in applicable circumstances
  • Object to certain processing
  • Request data portability where applicable
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection authority

The NDPC currently describes these as among the data subject rights under sections 34–38 of the NDPA.

17. How to Exercise Your Rights

To make a privacy request, contact Synth using the official email address listed below.

Please describe the request clearly and provide enough information for Synth to identify the relevant account or records.

For security, Synth may need to verify the identity or authority of the person making a request before disclosing, correcting or deleting personal information.

We will handle valid requests within the timeframe required by applicable law and may communicate where additional information or verification is needed.

18. Children and Minors

Synth services are intended to be used by individuals who are at least 18 years old, consistent with the eligibility requirements in Synth’s Terms & Conditions.

Synth does not knowingly seek to collect personal data from children through ordinary service ordering.

If a parent, guardian or other authorised person believes that a minor has provided personal data to Synth without an appropriate lawful basis, they may contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

19. Account and Password Security

Customers are responsible for keeping their login credentials confidential and for notifying Synth if they suspect unauthorised access to their account.

Synth may suspend access where reasonably necessary to protect the account, customer data, the website or other users.

20. Data Breach and Security Incidents

If Synth becomes aware of a personal data breach, we will assess and respond to the incident in accordance with applicable law and our security procedures.

Where notification to affected individuals, regulators or other parties is legally required, Synth will take reasonable steps to make the required notification within the applicable timeframe.

21. Third-Party Links

The Synth website or communications may contain links to third-party websites, platforms or services.

Those third parties operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Synth is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites that are outside our control.

22. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Synth may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, data practices, legal requirements or business operations change.

The updated version will be published through the appropriate Synth platform.

Where a change materially affects how personal data is processed, Synth will take reasonable steps to provide appropriate notice where required.

23. Complaints and Privacy Concerns

Customers and other data subjects are encouraged to contact Synth first with privacy concerns so that we can investigate and attempt to resolve the matter.

Nothing in this Policy prevents a data subject from exercising a lawful right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or another competent authority.
