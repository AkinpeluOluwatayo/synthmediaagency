5. SERVICE POLICY.

SYNTH MEDIA AGENCY // EFFECTIVE DATE: 17 SEPTEMBER 2026

1. Terms 2. Payment & Refund 3. Privacy 4. IP & Revisions 5. Services

1. Purpose and Scope

This Service Policy explains the operational standards, ordering requirements, lead times, production workflows, delivery mechanisms and service-specific conditions for services provided by Synth Media Agency (“Synth”, “we”, “us” or “our”).

This Policy forms part of Synth’s Terms & Conditions and applies to all services offered through our website, dashboard and digital platforms.

2. Services Covered

Synth provides digital media, creative, marketing and technology services. These services may include, but are not limited to:

  • Graphic Design Services
  • Video Content Creation and Video Marketing
  • Content Creation and Copywriting
  • Social Media Management
  • Business Branding Services
  • Digital Marketing and Advertising
  • Web Design and Development Services
  • Technology Solutions and Support
  • Custom digital, creative and technology packages

3. Service Ordering Process

Services must generally be ordered through the official Synth website or customer dashboard.

The standard process involves:

  1. Selecting the required service or package
  2. Providing complete project requirements and materials
  3. Submitting the order
  4. Making payment through the supported payment gateway (such as Paystack)
  5. Awaiting order confirmation
  6. Production and quality review
  7. Delivery of completed work through the customer dashboard

An order is not considered active for production purposes until payment has been successfully confirmed and the necessary brief/materials have been provided.

4. Project Brief and Requirements

Customers are required to provide clear, accurate and complete instructions when submitting an order.

Required information may include brand guidelines, logos, text copy, high-resolution images, video clips, colour preferences, reference examples, preferred formats, target audience details, domain names, hosting credentials or other information necessary to complete the project.

Synth is not responsible for production delays or errors resulting from vague, incomplete, inaccurate, conflicting or delayed instructions supplied by the customer.

5. Minimum Lead Time

Customers may specify a requested project deadline when placing an order.

The minimum requested lead time for any service is three (3) days from the date production commences.

Synth will not accept or guarantee a requested delivery time of less than three days unless Synth expressly agrees otherwise in writing.

The three-day minimum is a baseline minimum and does not mean that every project will automatically be delivered in three days.

Complex projects, large multi-page websites, custom software, extensive video editing, full branding suites, multi-asset marketing campaigns or orders requiring multiple revision rounds will naturally require longer production timelines.

6. Start of Production

Production timeline calculation begins only after ALL of the following conditions are met:

  • Payment is successfully processed and confirmed
  • The customer has submitted the complete project brief
  • All essential assets, materials and credentials required for the project have been received by Synth

If a customer delays supplying essential materials, the production start date and delivery schedule will be adjusted accordingly.

7. Service Timelines and Turnaround

Estimated turnaround times provided on package descriptions or order pages represent standard working estimates under normal operational conditions.

Actual turnaround times may vary depending on: Project complexity and scope; Customer response time for feedback or approvals; Revisions requested; Technical requirements or third-party platform dependencies; Volume of active orders in production.

Where a delay is anticipated, Synth will take reasonable steps to communicate with the customer.

8. Customer Responsibilities

To ensure successful project delivery, customers are responsible for:

  • Providing accurate project information and assets
  • Responding promptly to requests for clarification, feedback or approval
  • Ensuring supplied materials do not infringe third-party rights
  • Maintaining access to their registered email address and customer dashboard
  • Reviewing delivered work within the specified review period

9. Customer Delays

If a customer fails to provide required assets, clarification or feedback for a period exceeding 14 calendar days, Synth may place the project on hold.

If the delay exceeds 30 calendar days without communication, Synth reserves the right to mark the order as paused or completed based on work completed to date.

Reactivating a paused project may require rescheduling and may be subject to a reactivation fee where additional resources are required.

10. Revisions

Revisions are governed by the specific package allowance purchased by the customer and Synth’s Intellectual Property & Revision Policy.

A revision includes minor adjustments, corrections or refinements aligned with the original brief.

Revisions do not include complete redesigns, new concepts, new briefs or work outside the purchased package scope.

11. Review and Approval

Before final delivery, completed work may undergo internal quality review by Synth.

Work is then presented to the customer via the dashboard or email for review.

Upon receiving delivered work, the customer has seven (7) calendar days to review and either approve the deliverable or request revisions within their package allowance.

If no response or revision request is received within seven (7) calendar days, the deliverable may be deemed accepted and approved by the customer.

12. Delivery of Completed Work

Completed deliverables will generally be made available through the customer’s online dashboard for secure download.

Email notifications may be sent to inform the customer when a deliverable is ready.

Files will be delivered in standard industry formats specified in the package description (e.g., PNG, JPG, MP4, PDF, ZIP, HTML/CSS/JS files).

13. Download and Access

Customers are responsible for downloading and backing up their completed files promptly upon delivery.

While Synth strives to maintain dashboard availability, Synth is not an archival storage service and does not guarantee indefinite cloud hosting of completed customer files.

14. Graphic Design Services

Includes flyers, banners, social media graphics, posters, packaging designs and visual assets.

Deliverables will be provided in digital formats (e.g., PNG, JPG, PDF).

Print-ready setup is provided where specified in the package. Synth is not responsible for physical printing errors committed by external printing vendors.

15. Video and Content Creation Services

Includes video marketing, promo reels, motion graphics, AI-assisted video production and content creation.

Final videos are delivered in digital video formats (such as MP4).

Voiceover, stock footage, background music and specialized assets are included according to the package scope.

16. Branding Services

Includes logo design, brand identity suites, typography systems, colour palettes and brand guidelines.

Logo vector files (AI, EPS, SVG) are provided where included in the purchased branding package.

17. Web Design and Development

Includes website design, landing page creation, web solutions and front-end/back-end development.

Websites are developed to be responsive across modern devices and major web browsers.

Domain registration, web hosting, third-party software subscriptions, SSL certificates and ongoing website maintenance are the customer’s responsibility unless specifically included in the purchased service package.

18. Social Media Management

Includes content planning, post creation, scheduling, caption writing and social account management as defined in the selected package.

Customers must provide necessary account permissions or credentials required for post scheduling.

Synth is not responsible for account suspensions or restrictions imposed by social media platforms due to pre-existing account standing or external policy violations.

19. Digital Marketing and Advertising

Includes ad campaign setup, ad creative production, audience targeting and performance monitoring.

Ad spend budgets paid directly to advertising platforms (such as Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads) are separate from Synth’s service setup fees unless explicitly bundled.

Synth does not guarantee specific campaign conversion figures, ROI, follower counts or sales volume.

20. Technology Solutions and Support

Includes digital tool setup, automation workflows, AI tool integration, technical consultation and support services.

Technical support scope and hours are defined in the purchased service agreement.

21. Third-Party Platforms

Synth services may rely on third-party platforms (e.g., Paystack, Meta, Google, Vercel, Supabase, OpenAI, hosting providers).

Synth is not liable for service outages, policy modifications, algorithm updates or restrictions caused independently by third-party platforms.

22. Customer Accounts and Access

Customers must maintain secure passwords for their Synth dashboard and third-party services connected to their project.

23. Service Availability

Synth strives to maintain high availability of its website and customer dashboard, but temporary downtime for maintenance or updates may occur.

24. Service Limitations

Synth provides professional agency services but does not guarantee specific commercial outcomes, sales targets or platform growth metrics.

25. Prohibited or Unlawful Requests

Synth strictly refuses orders involving illegal content, fraudulent activities, deceptive marketing, hate speech or infringement of third-party rights.

26. Project Cancellation or Suspension by Synth

Synth reserves the right to suspend or cancel an order in cases of breach of terms, non-payment, unlawful requests or abusive behaviour towards staff.

27. Quality Concerns and Corrections

If a delivered service contains technical errors or fails to follow the agreed brief, the customer must notify Synth within the seven-day review window for prompt correction.

28. Customer Feedback

We welcome feedback to continuously improve our agency standards and service quality.

29. Exceptional Circumstances and Force Majeure

Synth is not liable for delays caused by national infrastructure failures, power outages, severe weather, government acts or major cyber incidents.

30. Relationship With Package Descriptions

Specific deliverable counts, feature lists and turnaround times defined in individual package descriptions take precedence over general defaults.

31. Relationship With Other Synth Policies

This Service Policy operates alongside Synth Media Agency’s Terms & Conditions, Payment & Refund Policy, Privacy Policy and IP & Revision Policy.

32. Changes to This Policy

Synth may update this Service Policy periodically. The effective date at the top of the policy indicates the date of the latest revision.
