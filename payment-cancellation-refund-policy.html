2. PAYMENT, CANCELLATION & REFUND POLICY.

SYNTH MEDIA AGENCY // EFFECTIVE DATE: 17 SEPTEMBER 2026

1. Terms 2. Payment & Refund 3. Privacy 4. IP & Revisions 5. Services

1. Purpose and Scope

This Payment, Cancellation & Refund Policy explains how payments, cancellations, refunds, payment disputes and related financial matters are handled when a customer purchases services from Synth Media Agency (“Synth”, “we”, “us” or “our”).

This Policy forms part of Synth’s Terms & Conditions.

2. Accepted Payment Method

Synth accepts online payments through the payment gateway provided on the Synth website.

The payment gateway currently used by Synth is Paystack.

Customers may be presented with the payment methods made available through Paystack at checkout.

3. When Payment Is Required

Full payment for the selected package is required before production or service delivery begins, unless Synth expressly agrees otherwise in writing.

Synth is not required to begin production while payment is incomplete, pending, failed or unconfirmed.

4. Package Pricing

The price displayed at checkout for the customer’s selected package is the applicable package price for that order, subject to any clearly disclosed taxes, fees or additional charges.

Prices may vary between packages and may be changed for future orders.

5. Payment Confirmation

An order proceeds to production only after Synth receives confirmation that the payment has been successfully completed.

A customer’s payment attempt, receipt of a bank debit notification, or payment status shown as pending does not by itself require Synth to begin production until the transaction is confirmed.

6. Failed, Pending or Reversed Payments

Where a payment is failed, pending, reversed, declined, disputed or otherwise not confirmed, Synth may place the order on hold.

Production will not begin until the required payment is successfully confirmed.

If a payment is later confirmed after an order has been placed on hold, the customer may contact Synth through the official support channel so the order can be reviewed.

7. Additional Services and Out-of-Scope Work

The customer’s payment covers the services and scope included in the purchased package.

Requests for work outside the purchased package, additional deliverables, extra revisions beyond the package allowance, expedited work, or other separately chargeable services may require additional payment.

Synth will communicate the applicable additional charge before carrying out chargeable additional work, where reasonably practicable.

8. Customer-Initiated Cancellation

Because Synth services may involve production, creative work, scheduling and allocation of resources after payment, customer-initiated cancellation after successful payment is generally not permitted.

This position is subject to any rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable Nigerian law.

9. Refunds and Refund Circumstances

Synth does offer refunds in appropriate circumstances; however, a refund is not automatically equal to the full amount paid.

Where production has started, Synth may deduct the reasonable value of work already completed and non-recoverable costs before calculating any refund, subject to applicable Nigerian law.

Where a refund is approved because Synth is unable to provide the paid service and the customer has not received the corresponding value, Synth may provide a full refund or an appropriate partial refund depending on the circumstances.

Where the customer requests cancellation after production has begun, any approved refund may be limited to the unused portion of the payment after deducting the value of work already performed and reasonable non-recoverable costs.

Where a customer has received a completed deliverable or substantial part of the contracted service, a refund may be reduced accordingly or may not be available for the portion already delivered, subject to applicable law.

Synth will not use this Policy to exclude any refund, remedy or consumer right that is required by applicable Nigerian law.

10. Refund Calculation Principle

To avoid treating every cancellation as an automatic full refund, Synth may assess an approved refund using the following principle:

Refund Amount = Amount Paid − Reasonable Value of Work Completed − Reasonable Non-Recoverable Costs (where applicable).

The exact amount will depend on the facts of the order and any rights or remedies required by law.

For clarity, Synth does not promise a fixed percentage refund for every situation.

A percentage-based refund may be used only as an administrative guideline where appropriate, and the final amount may depend on the stage of production, work completed, costs incurred, deliverables already supplied and applicable law.

11. Duplicate or Incorrect Payments

If a customer accidentally makes a duplicate payment for the same order, the customer should contact Synth promptly through the official support channel and provide the relevant order and payment information.

After verification, Synth may refund the verified duplicate amount, subject to payment-provider processing requirements.

If a customer pays the wrong amount or makes a payment that cannot be matched to an order, Synth will investigate the transaction and communicate the available resolution.

Customers should not send card details, PINs, passwords or other sensitive payment credentials to Synth by email or chat.

12. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

Customers are encouraged to contact Synth first through the official support channel before initiating a chargeback or payment dispute so that the transaction and order can be investigated and resolved where possible.

Synth reserves the right to provide relevant order, payment, delivery and communication records to the payment provider or financial institution when responding to a legitimate payment dispute, subject to applicable law and privacy obligations.

13. Payment Security

Payments are processed through the payment gateway used by Synth.

Where the payment provider handles card processing, Synth does not directly store customers’ full card details.

Customers should never send their card PIN, OTP, CVV, password or other confidential payment credentials to Synth staff.

Customers should complete payment only through the official Synth payment process or other payment method expressly authorized by Synth.

14. Third-Party Payment Providers

Synth may use third-party payment providers, including Paystack, to process payments.

Payment processing may be subject to the payment provider’s own terms, policies, security procedures, availability and technical requirements.

Synth is not responsible for delays or failures caused solely by a payment provider, bank, card issuer, telecommunications provider or other third party, although Synth will make reasonable efforts to assist where appropriate.

15. Pricing Changes

Synth may change package prices, service prices, discounts or promotional offers from time to time.

Pricing changes generally apply to future orders and do not change the price of an order that has already been successfully accepted and paid for, except where required by law or expressly agreed with the customer.

16. Taxes, Bank Charges and Transaction Fees

Applicable taxes, statutory charges or clearly disclosed transaction fees may apply to certain purchases where required or permitted.

Charges imposed by a customer’s bank, card issuer, mobile network, telecommunications provider or other third party are generally the customer’s responsibility unless applicable law or an express agreement provides otherwise.

17. Refund Processing

Where Synth approves a refund, the customer will be notified through the registered email address or customer dashboard where applicable.

Refunds will generally be processed through the original payment route or another appropriate method agreed by Synth and the customer.

The time required for the customer to receive a refund may depend on Paystack, the customer’s bank, card issuer, payment method or other financial institution.

Synth does not control the processing time imposed by external financial institutions.

18. Fraudulent or Suspicious Transactions

Synth may suspend, delay, review or cancel an order where there is reasonable evidence of fraudulent, suspicious, unauthorized or irregular payment activity.

Synth may request reasonable information necessary to verify a transaction.

Where appropriate, Synth may provide relevant information to payment providers, financial institutions or competent authorities, subject to applicable law.

19. Complaints and Payment-Related Issues

Customers with payment, cancellation or refund concerns should contact Synth through the official support channel.

The customer should provide the order/reference number, payment date, amount paid, payment reference where available, and a clear explanation of the issue.

Synth will review the matter and communicate the available resolution.

20. Relationship With Other Synth Policies

This Payment, Cancellation & Refund Policy should be read together with Synth Media Agency’s Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Intellectual Property & Revision Policy and Service Policy.

Where another policy addresses a matter specific to privacy, intellectual property, revisions or service delivery, that policy may provide the applicable additional rules.

21. Changes to This Policy

Synth may update this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in payment methods, business operations, services, technology, legal requirements or payment-provider arrangements.

The updated version will be published on the Synth website with an updated effective or revision date.
